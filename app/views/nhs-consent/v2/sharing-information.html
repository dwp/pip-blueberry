Sharing information about your health conditions and disabilities to support your application

The Department for Work and Pensions (DWP), or health professionals working on behalf of DWP, may need more information about:

  • your health conditions and disabilities and how they affect you
  • any medication you have been prescribed

With your consent, we can get this information from:

  • health professionals that you tell us about
  • your NHS record

How we use this information

We will only ask for or look at information that is relevant to this application. We use it to:

  • process your application
  • make a decision on your application and any mandatory reconsideration or appeal you make

The law allows us to obtain, keep and use this information.
