Difficulties dressing and undressing

For each difficulty tell us:

  • what the difficulty is
  • how often you have this difficulty – tell us about both good and bad days
  • why you have it, or how it relates to your condition
  • any aids or adaptations you use, or help you get from another person
  • about any help you feel you need but do not get

Examples

"About 3 times a week, I have trouble dressing my upper body because my hands shake too much to do up the buttons on my shirt. I use a button hook to do the buttons on my shirt."

"Most days my partner has to remind me to get dressed during the day."
